# AuditTrak — short pitch

A payment tells you money moved. It does not always explain the work behind it.

Imagine a freelancer completing a website. The original chat says 300,000 naira. The invoice says 350,000. A payment for 350,000 arrives. Is something wrong? Perhaps the client approved hosting later. A reviewer needs the supporting records to understand that difference.

AuditTrak gathers five parts of the job: the agreement, invoice, payment, delivery evidence, and client confirmation. It compares the supplied details with deterministic rules and explains what matches, what differs, and what remains missing. The business can then share the assessed record with a human reviewer.

Our local MVP includes saved business accounts, five fictional examples, evidence attachments, confirmation links, reviewer notes and an activity history. Bank import and AI extraction have separate provider boundaries; they are not live connections in this demo. AI proposals cannot rewrite the evidence or the rules.

Our first audience hypothesis is freelancers and small service businesses, alongside the reviewers examining their commercial records. We now want willing pilot users to test a consented job and tell us whether this reduces repeated evidence requests.

AuditTrak does not make credit decisions, label fraud, or prove a document is genuine. It helps a reviewer follow the commercial story and see the questions that still need answers.

## If asked about machine learning

We have not trained a machine learning model. The MVP first establishes a reliable evidence workflow and explicit comparisons. A future model would need consented, representative examples and reviewed labels, with evaluation against a defined task. Extraction and matching suggestions could assist people; the deterministic comparisons remain authoritative.

## If asked what is connected

No customer accounts are connected in the demo. The Mono adapter requires credentials and a consented account bound by the operator. AI extraction requires a configured HTTP provider and manually reviewed proposals. Marketplace, Gmail and Telegram artwork is fictional and does not demonstrate connectors to those services.

## If asked about revenue or customers

No customer traction, revenue, market size or validated pricing is claimed. A workspace or reviewer subscription is a business model hypothesis to test after understanding users and their review process.
