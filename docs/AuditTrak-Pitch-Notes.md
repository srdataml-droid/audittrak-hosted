# AuditTrak Pitch — speaker notes

## Slide 1

A bank line can show money moved. A reviewer may still need the agreement, invoice, delivery proof and client response. AuditTrak brings those records together.

## Slide 2

This problem framing comes from the project brief and fictional scenarios. Its frequency and willingness to pay still need customer research; no market statistics are claimed.

## Slide 3

These are proposed audiences, not confirmed customers. Direct client jobs are an initial focus because the owner can manually assemble records. Marketplace access and permitted data use need separate validation.

## Slide 4

The MVP supports this flow locally with persistent records, tenant separation, owner-controlled sharing and reviewer notes. It supports manual records; external services require separate setup.

## Slide 5

All figures and people are fictional. Each conversation illustration is a teaching scenario. Amina: matching amounts; Chidi: changed scope; Zainab: installment; Kemi: different payer; Tobi: linked debit. No fraud conclusion follows from any difference.

## Slide 6

This describes implemented constraints and remaining limitations, not an assurance of document authenticity or production security. Audit history is application-level and not tamper-proof.

## Slide 7

No live integrations, customers, revenue, pricing or trained machine learning model are claimed. Commercial pricing and the buyer must be tested; the subscription concept is a hypothesis.

## Slide 8

We are seeking a small set of willing freelancers and reviewers to try a consented evidence workflow. We want to learn what is missing, what is confusing, and whether a structured record saves repeated requests. We are not claiming lending outcomes or asking for a specific investment amount.
